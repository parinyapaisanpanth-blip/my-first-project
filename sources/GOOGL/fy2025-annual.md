# Alphabet FY2025 Annual Results (from Q4 2025 Earnings Release)

Source: SEC EDGAR — Alphabet Q4 & Full Year 2025 Earnings Release

---

## Revenue Summary

- **Total FY2025 revenue exceeded $400B for the first time** — exact figure from full 10-K; operating margin 31.6% for full year
- **Google Services** includes Search, YouTube ads, subscriptions, Google Play, devices — primary revenue driver
- **Google Cloud** revenues +48% YoY in Q4 2025, reaching $17.7B in Q4 alone
- **YouTube** revenue (ads + subscriptions combined) exceeded **$60B for full year 2025**

## Segment Operating Structure

Alphabet reports 3 segments:
1. **Google Services** — Search & other, YouTube ads, Google subscriptions/platforms/devices, Google Network
2. **Google Cloud** — GCP infrastructure, Google Workspace, enterprise AI services (revenue from consumption fees + subscriptions)
3. **Other Bets** — Waymo, Verily, etc.

## Key FY2025 Metrics (from earnings releases)

- Full-year **operating margin: 31.6%** — up from 27% in FY2024
- Google Cloud Q4 2025: $17.7B revenue, +48% YoY
- YouTube (full year): >$60B across ads and subscriptions
- Paid subscriptions (YouTube TV, YouTube Music/Premium, Google One, NFL Sunday Ticket): reached 350M+ by Q1 2026

## Capital Allocation

- Aggressive buyback program ongoing
- R&D investment heavy — Gemini models, TPU development, DeepMind
- CapEx significantly elevated for AI infrastructure build-out
